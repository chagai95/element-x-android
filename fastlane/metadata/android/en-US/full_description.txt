Matrix Chat — a personal Matrix client

Matrix Chat is a personal, rebranded build of Element X Android, pre-configured for the chagai.website homeserver. It gives you fast, secure and private instant messaging and video calls built on Matrix, the open standard for real-time communication.

 • Real time messaging & video calls
 • Public rooms for open group communication
 • Private rooms for closed group communication
 • Rich messaging features: emoji reactions, replies, polls, pinned messages and more
 • Video calling while browsing messages
 • Interoperability with other Matrix-based apps such as Element, FluffyChat, Cinny and many more
 • Call reminders: alarm-style, full-screen reminders to call someone back

<b>Privacy-first</b>
No data mining, no tracking, no analytics. End-to-end encryption by default.

<b>Own your conversations</b>
This build signs in to chagai.website by default, and you can still point it at any other Matrix homeserver from the sign-in screen.

<b>Open source</b>
Matrix Chat is a fork of Element X Android (https://github.com/element-hq/element-x-android) and is licensed under the AGPL-3.0. The complete corresponding source for this build is published at https://github.com/chagai95/element-x-android.
